Every frequency has **Repeat every N** (N is at least 1). N counts from the start date: days, ISO weeks (Monday to Sunday), months or years.

| Frequency | Options | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Optional: business days only | Every day at 08:00 |
| Weekly | One or more weekdays | Every 2 weeks on Monday and Thursday |
| Monthly, on a date | Day 1 to 31, or last day | The 15th of every month |
| Monthly, on a position | 1st to 4th or last of a weekday; 1st to 5th or last business day | The 2nd Tuesday; the last business day |
| Yearly | A month, then a date or a position | 1 March every year; the 1st business day of January |
| Advanced (cron or RRULE) | A cron expression or an RRULE line | Weekdays at 08:00 and 17:00 |

For anything the frequencies above cannot express, use an [advanced rule](#advanced-rules).

## In the Quick Start
The one-screen [Quick Start](getting-started.html) offers the common presets and **Custom: every X days, weeks, months or years**:
- **Days**: every X days (1 to 365), counted from the start date.
- **Weeks**: every X weeks (1 to 52) on one weekday. Weeks are counted from the week of the start date.
- **Months**: every X months (1 to 24) on a day of the month; 29 to 31 use the last day of shorter months.
- **Years**: every X years (1 to 10) on the month and day of the start date.

The Quick Start also asks **If it falls on a weekend**: **Keep the day** (default), **Move to the Friday before** or **Move to the Monday after**. It is the wizard's "When a date falls on a weekend or a holiday" policy (create anyway, previous business day, next business day), so public holidays of the holiday country move the same way, and a monthly move never leaves the month. **More options** opens the wizard with the same frequency, **Repeat every** value and policy.

## Examples
- **Every Monday**: Weekly, Monday.
- **Every 2 weeks**: Weekly, repeat every 2, pick the weekday.
- **Monthly report on the 1st business day**: Monthly on a position, 1st, Business day.
- **Month-end close**: Monthly on a position, Last, Business day. Optionally create 5 days early.
- **Quarterly**: Monthly, repeat every 3, on a date or position.
- **Every 3rd business day**: Daily, tick "Business days only", repeat every 3.

## Days that do not exist
Monthly on the 31st: pick what happens in a month without that day.
- **Use the last day of the month** (default): 31 April becomes 30 April.
- **Skip that month**: no issue that month.

Yearly on 29 February follows the same choice in non-leap years.

## Positions
- Weekday positions: 1st to 4th or last (for example "2nd Tuesday", "last Friday").
- Business-day positions: 1st to 5th or last business day of the month. A month with fewer business days than the position gives no issue that month.
- Business days depend on the [holiday calendar](holidays-and-business-days.html).

## Ending
Never, after N occurrences, or on a date. When a series ends the schedule status becomes **Ended**.

## Advanced rules
Choose **Advanced (cron or RRULE)** as the frequency when the other frequencies are not enough, for example two issues a day or "the last weekday of the month". You type a short rule in one of two common formats:
- **Cron**: five fields separated by spaces: minute, hour, day of the month, month, day of the week. `*` means "every", `1-5` is a range, `8,17` is a list and `*/2` means "every 2". Days of the week are 0 to 7 (0 and 7 are Sunday) or MON to SUN; months are 1 to 12 or JAN to DEC. The times come from the expression, so the **Time** field is hidden.
- **RRULE**: one line in the iCalendar format used by Google Calendar and Outlook, for example `FREQ=WEEKLY;BYDAY=MO`. Leave out `DTSTART` and `TZID`: the start date and the time zone are set in the wizard. Without `BYHOUR` the **Time** field gives the time; with `BYHOUR` and no `BYMINUTE` the minute is 00.

Examples (click them in the wizard to use them):

| You want | Cron | RRULE |
|---|---|---|
| Weekdays at 08:00 and 17:00 | `0 8,17 * * 1-5` | `FREQ=WEEKLY;BYDAY=MO,TU,WE,TH,FR;BYHOUR=8,17` |
| The 2nd Tuesday every 3 months | not possible in cron | `FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=TU;BYSETPOS=2;INTERVAL=3` |
| Monday and Wednesday at 09:00 and 15:00 | `0 9,15 * * 1,3` | `FREQ=WEEKLY;BYDAY=MO,WE;BYHOUR=9,15` |

The wizard checks the rule as you type and shows the next runs below it. The [Review step](wizard.html) shows the next 10.

Limits, to protect your Jira site:
- At most **24 issues a day**, and at least **60 minutes** between two issues. `*/15 * * * *` (every 15 minutes) is refused. A cron step larger than its field, such as `*/90` in the minute field, is refused too: cron cannot say "every 90 minutes".
- RRULE: `FREQ` must be `HOURLY` or slower (no `MINUTELY` or `SECONDLY`). `COUNT` and `UNTIL` work only when **Ends** is **Never**; otherwise use **Ends**. `BYYEARDAY`, `BYWEEKNO` and `BYSECOND` are not supported.
- Expressions are at most 200 characters.

Everything else works as for the other frequencies: the time zone and [clock changes](time-zones-and-dst.html), [holidays and business days](holidays-and-business-days.html), [early creation and exceptions](early-creation-and-exceptions.html), the start date and **Ends**. Good to know:
- In cron, when both the day of the month and the day of the week are set (neither starts with `*`), a day matching **either** one runs (standard cron). `0 9 1 * MON` runs on the 1st and on every Monday.
- A day that does not exist is not run: cron `0 8 31 * *` runs only in months with a 31st (use **Monthly, on a date** for "last day of the month").
- With a holiday policy that moves to a business day, each time keeps its time of day. Two runs that land on the same instant create one issue.
