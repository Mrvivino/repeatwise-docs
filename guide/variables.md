Use variables in the **Summary** and **Description** (the wizard lists them under the Summary field). They are filled in when each issue is created, in the schedule's time zone, in English.

| Variable | Result | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `{{date}}` | Creation date, `yyyy-MM-dd` | 2027-03-01 |
| `{{date:FORMAT}}` | Creation date, custom format | `{{date:dd/MM/yyyy}}` gives 01/03/2027 |
| `{{due}}` | Due date, `yyyy-MM-dd` | 2027-03-01 |
| `{{due:FORMAT}}` | Due date, custom format | `{{due:LLL d}}` gives Mar 1 |
| `{{month}}` | Month name of the creation date | March |
| `{{quarter}}` | Quarter of the creation date, `Q1` to `Q4` | Q1 |
| `{{year}}` | Year of the creation date | 2027 |
| `{{week}}` | ISO week number of the creation date | 9 |
| `{{day}}` | Day of the month of the creation date, `1` to `31` | 1 |
| `{{number}}` | Occurrence number, starting at 1 | 12 |
| `{{schedule}}` | The schedule name | Monthly close |

- `FORMAT` uses [Luxon tokens](https://moment.github.io/luxon/#/formatting?id=table-of-tokens): `yyyy`, `MM`, `dd`, `LLLL`, `EEE`, and so on. Up to 40 characters. Only `date` and `due` accept a format: for a two-digit day use `{{date:dd}}`.
- `{{date}}`, `{{day}}`, `{{month}}`, `{{quarter}}`, `{{year}}`, `{{week}}` follow the **creation** date, `{{due}}` the **due** date. They differ with early creation or a holiday move.
- `{{number}}` counts every date the rule produced, including dates that were skipped, so it can jump.
- Unknown variables and unusable formats are left as typed, so you can spot typos.
- The summary is trimmed to one line of at most 255 characters.

Example: `Close {{month}} {{year}} (due {{due:d LLL}})` created 21 Feb for a 26 Feb due date gives `Close February 2027 (due 26 Feb)`.

Example: `Access review {{quarter}} {{year}}` created 1 April 2027 gives `Access review Q2 2027`.
