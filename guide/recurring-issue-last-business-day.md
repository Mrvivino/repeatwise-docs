In RepeatWise, a monthly schedule can target the last business day or a weekday position such as the second Tuesday. Choose a holiday country and optional region in the wizard so business-day positions account for the public holidays included in that calendar.

## Set up a monthly rule

1. Open **RepeatWise** and select **New schedule**. For a simple month-end rule, enter the summary, project, and issue type in Quick Start and choose **Last business day of the month** under **Repeat**; it uses the default holiday country. To pick another holiday country or region, add extra holidays, or use a different monthly position, select **More options** to open the wizard.
2. In **When**, choose **Monthly, on a position**. For month-end work, set **Which** to **Last** and **Day** to **Business day**; for an Nth weekday, choose **1st** through **4th**, or **Last**, and the weekday. Set the time, time zone and start date. Use **Repeat every** to space the rule by months, such as 3 for every 3 months.
3. In **Rules**, select a holiday country and, if relevant, a region. For example, choose **France** for French public holidays. Add company closure dates as **Extra holidays**, or mark a listed holiday as a workday with **Removed holidays**. Saturdays and Sundays remain weekends.
4. If the rule targets a calendar date rather than a business-day position, choose what to do when that date is a weekend or holiday: create anyway, move to the previous or next business day, or skip it. A **Last business day** position already lands on a business day, so this policy does not move it.
5. In **Review**, compare the next 10 dates and the example issue with your expected calendar, then select **Save**. For example, France's last business day of February 2027 is Friday 26 February because the 28th is a Sunday.

## FAQ

### Is the last business day the same as the last day with a move policy?

They can produce the same date, but they are different rules. **Last business day** chooses a business day directly; a last-calendar-day rule uses the holiday policy to adjust its due date.

### Which weekday positions are available?

For monthly weekday rules, choose **1st** through **4th** or **Last**. Business-day positions offer **1st** through **5th** or **Last**; see [Frequencies and examples](frequencies.html).

### Do all French non-working days count automatically?

No. The calendar includes public holidays covered by the bundled data; bank-only, optional, school and observance days do not count, and neither do half-day holidays. Add other dates as **Extra holidays** and verify the preview. See [Holidays and business days](holidays-and-business-days.html).
