## Early creation
On the **When** step, "Create the issue this many days early".
- 0 (default): created on the due date.
- 5: created 5 calendar days before the due date, at the same time of day. Due Friday 26 February at 08:00, created Sunday 21 February at 08:00.
- The early date is not moved to a business day. Only the due date follows the holiday policy.
- Summary variables: `{{date}}`, `{{day}}`, `{{month}}`, `{{quarter}}`, `{{year}}` and `{{week}}` use the creation date, so created on 21 February shows February. `{{due}}` shows the due date. See [Variables](variables.html).
- The Jira due date field is set only if the template has a relative due date (not in the wizard yet).

## Exceptions
In **Review**, use **Skip** or **Move** beside a previewed occurrence. Move sets a new due date and, optionally, a new time. Leave the time empty to keep the usual time. The holiday policy is not applied to the moved date. The exception list has **Undo**.

An exception applies to one original occurrence. If you change the rule or time, it may no longer match. A skipped occurrence still uses an occurrence number.
