Create your first recurring issue on one screen.

1. Open **RepeatWise** from the Jira apps menu or a project's sidebar.
2. Select **New schedule**. If the list is empty, select **Create a schedule**.
3. Under **What do you want to repeat?**, type a sentence, or select an example under **Try an example**. To skip the sentence, switch the toggle from **Natural language** to **Form**; RepeatWise remembers your choice in this browser.
4. Check the form card. **Issue** is the issue summary (select the pencil to edit it); the issue type is shown below it, with **Change** to pick another. Choose a **Project**. Under **Repeat**, choose **Every day**, **Every weekday**, **Every week**, **Every 2 weeks**, **Every month**, **Last business day of the month**, **Every quarter**, **Every year**, or **Custom: every X days, weeks, months or years**, then the weekday or day of the month when prompted, and the **Starts** date. Set **Time** and **Time zone** (an IANA time zone).
   - **Custom**: type X under **Every** (1 to 365 days, 1 to 52 weeks, 1 to 24 months, 1 to 10 years) and pick the unit. Weeks run on the chosen weekday; months on the chosen day of the month (29 to 31: shorter months use their last day); years on the month and day of the start date. The start date anchors the cycle, and the sentence says so: "every 3 months on the 15th at 09:00 (Europe/Paris), starting 15 Oct 2026". Weeks are counted from the week of the start date, so a weekday earlier in that week waits for the next cycle.
   - **If it falls on a weekend**: **Keep the day** (default), **Move to the Friday before**, or **Move to the Monday after**. When a run moves, public holidays of the default holiday country move the same way (the help text names the country). For monthly rules a move never leaves the month: on the 1st, "Friday before" moves forward instead. **Every weekday** and **Last business day of the month** never land on a weekend, so they do not show this choice.
5. Under **If previous issue is still open**, choose **Create anyway**, **Skip this occurrence**, or **Create and comment on the previous one**. To move the previous issue to a status instead, use **More options**.
6. Check **Next runs**: the next three dates with their time, and the rule, for example "And every Monday at 09:00 (Europe/Paris)". A run moved by the weekend rule says where it came from, for example "Fri 13 Nov · moved from Sat 14".
7. Select **Create schedule**. **Every weekday** and **Last business day of the month** use the default holiday country (your administrator's setting, or one detected from your browser language): **Every weekday** skips holidays, and the last business day avoids them. The other frequencies use it only when the weekend rule moves runs. To choose another holiday calendar, an end rule, a description, sub-tasks, a sprint, or other advanced fields, select **More options** to open the [4-step wizard](wizard.html) with your entries filled in.

**Quick templates** at the bottom shows **Weekly status report**, **Sprint retrospective**, and **Monthly close**; select **Use a template** to see all of them, including **Daily checks**, **Quarterly access review**, and **Yearly renewal**. A template prefills the issue summary, a description, and the timing. **Monthly close** creates each issue 5 days early, and **Quarterly access review** starts on the first day of the next calendar quarter. Check the project, issue type, time, time zone, and start date before creating the schedule.

You can also use **Make this issue recurring** on a Jira issue to prefill supported fields.

## Describe it in one sentence

In **Natural language** mode, the **What do you want to repeat?** box accepts an English or French sentence and fills the Quick Start form. Select **×** to clear it. For example:

- `Weekly report every Monday at 9am`
- `Monthly close on the last business day at 5pm`
- `Rapport hebdomadaire tous les lundis à 9h`
- `Clôture le dernier jour ouvré du mois à 17h`
- `Backup check every 3 days` or `tous les 3 jours`
- `Invoice review every 2 months on the 15th` or `tous les 2 mois le 15`

**Understood** shows what RepeatWise recognized; **Not understood** identifies anything it could not use. Words describing the work can fill an empty issue summary. Review and edit the fields before selecting **Create schedule**. The sentence is parsed in your browser and is not sent to an AI service.

RepeatWise checks for due schedules every 5 minutes. You need the project's **Create issues** permission and, by default, project administrator access. Issues are created as the schedule owner. See [Permissions](permissions.html).

If your site has Rovo, you can also ask the **RepeatWise** agent in Rovo chat to suggest recurring work, create a schedule after confirmation, or list the schedules you can see. See [RepeatWise in Rovo chat](rovo.html).
