Each schedule has its own IANA time zone (for example `Europe/Paris`). It defaults to your time zone, or to the administrator's default if one is set.

- The rule ("every Monday at 08:00") is computed in the schedule's zone, so 08:00 stays 08:00 on the wall clock all year.
- Instants are stored in UTC internally. The lists and previews show times in the schedule's zone.
- Changing your own Jira time zone does not move existing schedules.

## Clock changes
| Situation | What RepeatWise does | Example (Paris) |
|---|---|---|
| The time does not exist (spring forward) | Uses the first valid minute after the gap | 02:30 on 2027-03-28 becomes 03:00 |
| The time happens twice (fall back) | Uses the first occurrence, once only | 02:30 on 2027-10-31 is created once, at the earlier 02:30 |

The Review step notes such days. Only the creation time moves: the due date is a calendar date and is not changed by a clock change.

## When a country changes its time rules
Governments sometimes change their clocks for good, for example Morocco, which moved to permanent GMT on 20 September 2026. RepeatWise follows the official time zone database (IANA) and applies such changes itself, even before Atlassian's servers or your browser have them, so the schedule, the lists and the exports keep the same wall clock time. A schedule set for 09:00 in Casablanca runs at 09:00 Casablanca time.

## Accuracy
The scheduler wakes up every 5 minutes. An issue is created within 5 minutes of its target time, not at the exact second. If Jira or the app is briefly unavailable, see [Errors](errors.html) for retries and catch-up.
