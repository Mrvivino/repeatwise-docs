# RepeatWise

RepeatWise creates Jira issues on a repeating schedule: every Monday, on the last business day of the month, every quarter, and so on. It is a Jira Cloud app built on Atlassian Forge and published by Cocoon Care LLC.

- Set up a schedule in a 4-step wizard.
- Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly rules, computed in the schedule's own time zone.
- Business-day and public-holiday aware.
- Built on Atlassian Forge: your data stays in the Atlassian platform and RepeatWise makes no external network calls. Eligible for Runs on Atlassian (all versions deployed so far); the badge itself is not claimed until Atlassian grants it.

RepeatWise will be available on the Atlassian Marketplace soon.
